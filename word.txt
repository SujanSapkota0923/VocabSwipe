VocabSwipe Feature Enhancements
This plan outlines the implementation of two major features to enhance the learning experience in VocabSwipe: a Spaced Repetition System (SRS) and Enhanced Card Content (including example sentences and audio pronunciations).

Proposed Changes
1. Spaced Repetition System (SRS)
Currently, words are only marked as "Known" or "Don't Know". An SRS will use the SuperMemo-2 (SM-2) algorithm to schedule reviews at optimal intervals, significantly improving long-term retention.
